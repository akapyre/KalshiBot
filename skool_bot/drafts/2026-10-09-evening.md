# Hot Take: You Planned The Start. You Never Planned The Stop.

There's a piece floating around today about the one question doctors say most patients forget to ask before they come off Ozempic or Wegovy. I'm not going to pretend I know the exact question they picked, but I'll tell you what I think about every single time someone messages me about month 9 on a GLP-1. So let's talk about it.

Most of you spent WEEKS researching how to start. Which compound, what the trials showed, what titration looks like, what the side effects are. Good. That's the work.

Then you ask me "what happens when I stop?" and there's just... silence. No plan. No timeline. No idea what your body is going to do when the signal goes away.

That's backwards, and here's why it matters.

A GLP-1 is not a cure, it's a SIGNAL. It's holding a lever down for you. Appetite suppression, slowed gastric emptying, changed food reward, all of it is pharmacology doing a job your behavior wasn't doing on its own. The second that lever comes back up, your hunger comes back. That's not failure, that's the drug doing exactly what it was designed to do and then leaving.

So if the only thing that changed over your run was the number on the scale, you didn't change anything. You rented a result.

WHAT AN ACTUAL EXIT PLAN LOOKS LIKE

1. Did you build eating habits you can run WITHOUT appetite suppression? If your "diet" was just not being hungry, that's not a diet. That's a side effect.

2. Did you train and eat protein the whole way through? Weight loss is never pure fat. This is the entire reason companies are now testing muscle-sparing agents alongside GLP-1s, because lean mass loss on these drugs is a real, known problem, not a bro myth.

3. Do you know what you're monitoring? People will drop serious money on vials and ZERO dollars on labs. That's the part that actually bugs me.

4. Did you talk to a prescriber about how coming off is handled? Stopping is a medical decision with the same weight as starting, and most people treat it like canceling a subscription.

5. Do you know what you'll do if the weight starts coming back? Because for a lot of people it does, and the ones who spiral are always the ones who never considered it was possible.

I'm not saying this to scare anyone off GLP-1s. They're some of the most impressive compounds we've ever had, and I talk about them constantly. I'm saying it because "I'll figure it out later" is the single most common plan I hear, and later always shows up faster than you think.

If you're on something right now, ask yourself honestly: do you have an off-ramp, or do you have a hope? Those are not the same thing.

And if you don't have one yet, I'm not mad at you. Just start building it today instead of on your last pen. Drop your thoughts below, I want to hear how you guys are thinking about the back half of your run.
