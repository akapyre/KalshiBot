How to Actually Read a Study

You've probably seen me post breakdowns of studies on compounds and wonder "How do you actually read this stuff?" because PubMeds can be confusing.

For more complex stuff, you can use AI, but I don't let it think for me and you shouldn't let it think for you either. There's a difference, and it matters a lot in a space with a ton of misinfo.

1. Get the abstract first, not the headlines

The first part of every study is going to be the abstract. This is where they break down the thesis and goals of the article. If it's a more complexing title, use AI to help summarize it like your a middle schooler.

2. Break it into chunks — don't try to swallow it whole

Abstracts pack 5-6 dense claims into a paragraph. Instead of reading top to bottom, isolate one sentence at a time and ask: "What is this sentence actually claiming?"

Example from a 9-Me-BC study I broke down recently:
"9-me-BC exerted anti-proliferative effects without toxic properties in dopaminergic midbrain and cortical astrocyte cultures."

Broken down:

* Anti-proliferative = slowed cell division
* Without toxic = not killing/damaging the cells
* Translation: it told support cells to chill on multiplying, without harming them

3. Ask AI to explain mechanism, not implications

This is the key move. Ask "what does this term mean" and "what is this pathway", not "does this work" or "should I take this." AI is great at translating PI3K pathways or IC50 values into plain English. It's not a substitute for knowing the actual clinical evidence, dosing safety, or regulatory status of a compound.

4. Ask "what would make this NOT true"

Ask AI to help you find the caveats the authors included: small sample size, animal model only, no human trials, funding source, etc. If a summary you're reading has zero limitations mentioned, that's a red flag that you're reading a marketing summary, not the study.

Peptide and longevity content is saturated with people quoting studies they've never actually read, stripped of context, stacked into a sales pitch. Using AI to build genuine comprehension is absolutely fine to help you understand. Instead of using AI to generate a summary you paste and never verify.

The compound is only ever as good as your understanding of why it does what it does. That's the whole point of doing this the slow way.
