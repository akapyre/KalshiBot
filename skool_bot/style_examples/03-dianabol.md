Dianabol - The FULL Guide To AAS

(D-Bol) / Metandienone One of the original mass-building compounds, and still one of the most talked about mass building compounds today, so let's talk about it.

It's origin starts in the 1950s in the U.S., This was largely to help American Olympic athletes compete against Soviet athletes who were already using testosterone. Lets just say it works.

Dianabol is a testosterone derivative that's been 17-alpha alkylated so it survives the liver and can be taken orally, but that same modification is what makes it hepatotoxic (hard on liver enzymes).

Metabolism & Side Effects

* Metabolized by both 5-alpha reductase and aromatase
* Low affinity for 5-AR → fewer androgenic sides (hair loss, prostate issues)
* High affinity for aromatase → more estrogenic sides: gyno risk, water retention, "moon face"

Effects

Dianabol is known for it's fast strength and size gains, along with noticeable pumps, a big reason it's historically been a favorite for quick, visible results. The real problem is the bloat.

Approval was pulled in 1980. It remains a Schedule III controlled substance in the U.S., though compounding pharmacies can legally produce it under specific circumstances.
