How You're Going To Optimize You're Sleep

I made a tiktok this afternoon on peptides for sleep, but fixing the root cause and sleep hygiene is so important, so let's talk about how you can work to fix your sleep schedules.

The fixable stuff, in order of impact:

1. Consistent wake time — more important than bedtime. Your circadian rhythm anchors off when you get up, not when you go down.
2. Light exposure — 5/10 min of sunlight within an hour of waking; dim/warm light after sunset. This will help both mood and circadian rythm. Look it up for yourself!
3. Temperature — cool room (65-68°F), warm shower/bath 1-2hrs before bed accelerates the drop in core temp that triggers sleep onset. One of my favorite compounds is VIP before bed, to help with internal temperature and winding down.
4. Caffeine cutoff — half-life is 5-6 hours, quarter-life is longer. That 2pm coffee is still in your system at 10pm. I personally dont take preworkout or energy drinks past 4pm.
