Missed a dose? Here's what to actually do!

Everybody misses a dose eventually. Here's how you should actually approach that missed dose.

1. Don't panic, and never double up.

One missed dose won't wreck your protocol. Doubling the next dose to "catch up" is how people end up with side effects.

2. Check the half-life.

* Short half-life, daily (BPC-157, ipamorelin, CJC, etc.): Skip it and resume your normal schedule. Nothing to make up.

* Weekly GLP-1s: Simply take the next day and readjust your schedule based off the new dosing. ABSOLUTELY do not double up.

3. Resume, don't restart.
Unless you've been off for a long stretch (especially with GLP-1s, where you may need to re-titrate), just pick up where you left off.

4. Log it.
Note the miss in your tracker so you can connect any changes in how you feel to the gap!

5. Fix the cause.
Missed doses are usually a routine problem. Set a phone alarm, tie the dose to something you already do daily, or prep syringes the night before.

Overall, freaking out should be the last thing you do. Everyone will eventually miss their dose in their pep journey, stay calm and cool! It'll be alright!
