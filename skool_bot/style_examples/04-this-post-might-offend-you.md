This Post Might Offend You...

I'm going to say something that's going to rub some people the wrong way. If you're running a peptide because you saw it on TikTok, and you took no time to research before hand, you need to reevaluate how you research.

I get it. New compounds, new stacks, new claims every week. It's tempting to just copy what the guy with the good physique is doing or the person who claims they have the "best energy", but you are STILL injecting research chemicals into your body.

Peptides aren't magic. They're signaling molecules that interact with real receptors & real pathways, Dosing matters. Timing matters. Sourcing matters.
One of the thing that frustrates me the most, is when people don't even understand what the compound does or what dosing looks like before buying it. I have seen anaphylactic reactions, pancreatitis, nausea, heartaches, fainting, all because the proper research wasn't taken.

Now, if this is you, I'm not mad at you, we just need to switch up our approach. Take the time to study and educate yourself before buying the compound. If you're someone who just buys whatever I talk about, I appreciate you putting your trust in me. But please always do your own continuous research outside of what I say, these compounds aren't one size fits all!
